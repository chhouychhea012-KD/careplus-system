Help & guidance

Using your hospital workspace safely.

Account access

Contact your administrator for an account, role changes or recovery email updates. Administrators can create and deactivate staff accounts while preserving their audit history.

Password recovery

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page. Eligible accounts receive a single-use email link when SMTP recovery is configured. Links expire after 30 minutes. If no message arrives, contact your administrator.

Keep your session secure

Sign out when leaving a shared computer. Sessions time out after 20 minutes of inactivity. Password changes, resets and access changes revoke existing sessions. Repeated failed sign-ins temporarily restrict attempts for 15 minutes.
